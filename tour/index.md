The Recovering Auraria's Past Walking Tour is an audio tour of the historic Auraria neighborhood in Denver, Colorado. Written and produced by the project eam and in collaboration with displaced Aurarian community members, the tour uses narration and oral history excerpts to tell the story of this neighborhood and the community that was displaced.

The tour consists of 12 stops across the Auraria campus. You can listen to the full tour as a single episode, explore individual stops below, or subscribe on your favorite podcast platform to listen while walking the neighborhood.
