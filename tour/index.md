"Listening to Auraria's Past" is an audio walking tour of the historic Auraria neighborhood in Denver, Colorado. Written and produced by the project team in collaboration with displaced Aurarian community members, the tour uses narration and oral history excerpts to tell the story of this neighborhood and the community that was displaced. The walking tour consists of eleven stops located on the Auraria campus. You can walk along while listening to these stops on your favorite podcast app or access them through our website.
